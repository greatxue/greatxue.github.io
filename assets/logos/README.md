# Experience logo sources

These files are served locally. Logos retain their original proportions and colors.

- `microsoft-wordmark-hires.png`: original 2096 × 771 PNG from the [Microsoft News Center photo library](https://news.microsoft.com/microsoft-news-center-photos/). [Image](https://msftstories.thesourcemediaassets.com/2014/10/MSFT_logo_rgb_C-Gray.png). The image includes white margins; CSS scales the whole source without cropping it.
- `bytedance-wordmark-en.svg`: original color icon and English wordmark, decoded from the inline SVG in the [ByteDance website](https://www.bytedance.com/?locale=en) [JavaScript bundle](https://lf-package-sg.feishucdn.com/obj/atsx-throne-sg/hire-fe-prod/bytedance_official_website/static/js/main.31b2dc0fd6.js). Artwork and colors are unchanged.
- `yale-wordmark.svg`: original paths from the [Yale website's SVG sprite](https://www.yale.edu/sites/all/themes/yale_blue/images/icons.svg), with its viewport restricted to the 169 × 76 region specified by [Yale's stylesheet](https://www.yale.edu/sites/all/themes/yale_blue/css/site.css). No paths or colors were redrawn.
- `neurips.svg`: unmodified official SVG from the [NeurIPS Media Kit](https://neurips.cc/public/MediaKit). [Original SVG](https://neurips.cc/media/Press/NeurIPS_logo.svg), with intrinsic dimensions 59.671822 × 26.738739. The [2025 call for papers](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers) lists author notification as September 18, 2025 (AoE); the [2025 conference page](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025) lists San Diego, California, December 2–7, 2025.
- `neurips-icon.svg`: unmodified 36 × 36 compact square SVG used in the navigation of the [official NeurIPS 2025 website](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025). [Original SVG](https://neurips.cc/static/core/img/neurips-navbar-logo.svg). The artwork includes small NeurIPS lettering. No cropping, redrawing, or color changes.
- `corl.png`: unmodified 834 × 834 PNG served as the favicon of the [official CoRL 2026 website](https://2026.corl.org/). [Original image](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/sitesv-images-rt/APXyLQeB1RCKwUYFvylVj6wUlv5XYwh4h-o-e4xGV3AuhtYZK6QIASAPJV-NxkZP0Ulw7H6jUeM0IaSfIn_wDGi08PTrVSHxRCkrlmFcuL6ykFgYkgOMhK7bYuD7XdjiftiLAnEFStpiQj235aoafBYxsrosfHDCE6KoNvnP8p7bFTTAlBVY4HlNY7m1YEh5eIsnKLahZ968iUQW0qIw9rdVR2aoNg). The complete circular skyline-and-circuit artwork is retained without cropping, redrawing, or color changes. The embedded Key Dates table on the [author instructions page](https://2026.corl.org/contributions/instruction-for-authors) lists paper acceptance notifications as September 4, 2026, 23:59 AoE; this is the conference-wide scheduled notification date, not a verified individual notification timestamp.

The experience list uses the existing CASIA and CUHK-Shenzhen emblems without added captions. Yale uses its original wordmark. Small screens retain compact emblems.
